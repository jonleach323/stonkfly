      HOW IT WORKS.

      A real wiring diagram. Approximate neurons. An engineered game interface.

      1. 01 / INPUT21 TILES · 1 BOARD

        THE FLY GETS THE PIXELS.

        Each round the live board becomes a 320×180 image. Its pixels stimulate 3,335 brightness inputs and 811 R8 color inputs. The fly sees pixels, not odds.

      2. 02 / NETWORK25.6M CONNECTIONS

        THE NETWORK FIRES.

        MaleCNS v1.0 wires 166,700 simulated cells. Approximate electrical dynamics make them spike, and spikes spread down the wiring to the descending neurons.

      3. 03 / ACTIONSOLANA · DEPLOYPUBLIC

        A SPIKE CAN BECOME A DEPLOY.

        A fixed readout turns descending-neuron spikes into tiles. A guard checks timing, budget and count. It can veto, never choose. At most one deploy per round.

      4. 04 / FEEDBACKCANDIDATE MEMORY

        THE ROUND FEEDS BACK.

        When a round settles, a hit fires a 200 ms pulse into 15 PAM11 dopamine cells and a miss fires it into two PPL101 cells. Money is not the signal: fees make even a hit a small loss. A candidate memory rule changes selected connections.
