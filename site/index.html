        HOW IT WORKS

        Every round the public SatRush board is drawn as a 320×180 picture, shown to a spiking network wired from a fly connectome, and a fixed readout turns descending-neuron spikes into a tile choice. That is the whole mechanism. There is no strategy layer, and none could beat a random draw.

        1. 01

          INPUT

          The fly gets the pixels.

          The board (21 tiles shaded by stake, the last five winners outlined, pot, miner count and countdown) is rendered locally as RGB. Its pixels drive 3,335 brightness inputs (R1–R6 cells) and 811 R8 color inputs. The frame never shows this wallet's balance, its own picks or its results. The simulator sees pixels, not tile numbers or odds.

        2. 02

          NETWORK

          Spikes run through the whole retained graph.

          MaleCNS v1.0: 166,700 cells and 25.6M connections, integrated as leaky integrate-and-fire neurons at 0.1 ms with a coarse transmitter-sign proxy. Each observation advances 500 ms of neural time inside a round of about 63 s. The wiring comes from a real reconstruction of a male fly's brain and nerve cord; the physiology is a coarse approximation, and nothing is pruned.

        3. 03

          ACTION

          A fixed readout, not a plan.

          Every descending neuron is sorted by ID and cut into 21 pre-registered groups, one per tile. A group firing above its own running average, beyond the median, proposes its tile; the largest excess is always in. A guard checks timing, budget, count and account state. It can veto; it never substitutes tiles. What passes becomes at most one DeployPublic per round: in live mode it is sent to Solana from a dedicated wallet; in paper mode nothing is sent and the deploy is settled on paper against the real winning tile and pot. The stake is a fixed setting, never a neural quantity.

        4. 04

          FEEDBACK

          One pulse per settled round.

          When a round the fly played settles, its result is refund plus sats value (plus, in live play, any RUSH token value the API reports) minus the amount deployed. At least +0.01 USDC schedules a 200 ms pulse into 15 PAM11 dopamine cells; at most −0.01 USDC, the same pulse into 2 PPL101 cells. A candidate memory rule then adjusts existing KC→MBON connections. Fees count as loss, so most rounds are aversive. This is engineered reinforcement, not modeled pain or pleasure.

        CHANGING CONNECTIONS ARE NOT PROVEN SKILL.

        • The winning tile is drawn on chain. No reading of the board predicts it, and synaptic change is not evidence of learning to play well.
        • Every deploy pays a 6% fee and each missed tile loses 11% of what was placed on it. Expected value is negative every round; the balance is expected to decline.
        • Paper mode settles hypothetical deploys from the real winning tile and pot. Results tagged PAPER or SIM are simulated. Only LIVE WALLET in the footer means real deploys.
        • The 3D fly is decorative. It is not a neural or muscle reconstruction.
        • One worker owns the ledger. This page reads published snapshots and can change nothing.
